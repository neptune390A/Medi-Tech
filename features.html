Key Features

  • Hybrid WhatsApp & NFC Check-In: Patients can check in digitally through WhatsApp or physically using NFC cards, ensuring speed, accessibility, and zero app downloads.
  • Secure Digital Patient Profiles: All patient data is encrypted and stored securely with proper access control and legally valid digital consent.
  • Automated Insurance Verification: Insurance details are validated during check-in, reducing billing mistakes, claim rejections, and administrative delays.
  • Guided Department Assignment: Smart workflows guide receptionists through admissions, reducing errors and ensuring patients reach the correct department faster.
  • Seamless Hospital Integration: MediForm Pro integrates with existing hospital information and billing systems with minimal retraining for staff.
