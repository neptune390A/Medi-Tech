MediForm Pro

MediForm Pro is a hybrid hospital check-in and patient intake system designed to eliminate paperwork, reduce waiting times, and simplify front-desk operations. It combines WhatsApp-based digital intake with hospital-issued NFC cards to ensure accessibility for all patients.

Unlike traditional hospital systems that rely on manual forms and repetitive data entry, MediForm Pro allows patient information, consent, and insurance details to be securely verified and displayed instantly at reception.

The system is built for real hospital environments — including senior citizens, repeat visitors, and emergency cases — without requiring patients to download any mobile application.
