Why & How MediForm Pro Works

Why Hospitals Need MediForm Pro

Hospitals today struggle with long queues, excessive paperwork, staff overload, and patient dissatisfaction. Manual intake systems slow down admissions and increase the risk of data errors and compliance issues.

MediForm Pro addresses these challenges by digitizing patient intake while remaining inclusive for non-tech users, senior citizens, and emergency scenarios.

How It Works

Patients check in using WhatsApp before arrival or tap a hospital-issued NFC card at reception. Their verified information, consent, and insurance details appear instantly on the receptionist’s screen.

How It Can Be Implemented

MediForm Pro integrates seamlessly with existing hospital systems and workflows. Staff training is minimal, allowing hospitals to adopt the solution quickly without disrupting daily operations.
